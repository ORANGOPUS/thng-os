What's a node?

Every device you own is a node. THNG wires them into one space.

Your laptop renders the scene, your headset steps into it and your watch nudges a panel along. Each device runs the THNG node, which offers whatever that device can actually do and nothing it can't.

01 · NodeThe runtime on each device. It detects the screen, input, capture and XR the device supports.
02 · SceneThe space you're in: sky, platform, lighting, sound and layout rules.
03 · PanelAnything with pixels, such as a window, a monitor, a VM, a stream or a widget.
04 · ServiceBuilt-in apps: FORG, thngPlay, Spatial Desktop and Rogue Protocol.

Questions

Will there ever be a paid version?

No. THNG and its services are MIT-licensed. Sponsorship is optional and doesn't change what you get.

Can I fork it, sell it or ship it on my hardware?

Yes. The MIT license allows commercial use, modification and redistribution. Just keep the license notice.

Why are the Windows and macOS builds unsigned?

Signing certificates cost money. Sponsorship is the plan for getting the builds signed and notarized.

Architecture

Each device runs a node. Nodes discover each other on the local network and agree on one scene. The node that renders that scene holds the panels, and the others are inputs, mirrors or companions. Services are panel providers that every node can open.

LayerOwnsBuilt on
NodeCapability detection, discovery, updatesElectron / Capacitor shells from Spatial Desktop
SceneSky, platform, lighting, audio, layout rulesIWSDK + WebXR, shaders generated in code
PanelPixels + input passthroughPipeWire capture, wayvnc, VNC (VMs)
ServiceApps: FORG, thngPlay, Spatial Desktop, Rogue ProtocolWeb panels

Capabilities, not promises

At startup a node checks what its device can do and offers only that. The UI never shows a control the device can't use. It shows the reason instead, for example "Window capture needs Linux with Hyprland."

Colour

Colour comes from the Once UI library, themed with data-theme="dark", data-neutral="gray", data-brand="aqua" and data-accent="violet". Aqua (THNG cyan) signals interaction and availability, and violet marks planned or secondary things. Status always comes with a text label and a shape.

Motion

Motion explains a change in state. Durations come from Once UI's transition tokens. Small interactions take 150–200ms with ease-out on enter, and exits run at about 60% of that with ease-in. Ambient motion in the scene stops when prefers-reduced-motion is set.
